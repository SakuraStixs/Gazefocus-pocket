GazeFocus Pocket

Pick a window with your eyes, using your front camera

A phone-sized taste of GazeFocus: the same idea as the desktop app, with four big windows instead of two monitors. Everything runs on your phone; no video leaves it.

  1. Hold the phone at eye level, about 30 cm away, in good light from the front.
  2. Follow 13 dots with your eyes, keeping your head fairly still. It also learns your eye shape.
  3. Look at a window for half a second and it takes focus. Tap anywhere you're looking to teach it.

Expect window-level accuracy: on a phone screen it can tell which quarter you're looking at, not exact spots.
